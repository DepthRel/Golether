You were invited

Four steps to the film

All you need is Golether on your computer and the link your friend sent you.

  1. Click the link

    Your browser asks whether to open Golether. Say yes.

  2. Confirm

    Golether shows the name of the session and who invites you. Press “Join”.

  3. Compare the code

    You and the host see the same three words and a number. Say them to each other aloud.

    mapleriverstone42

  4. Enjoy

    The host lets you in, and the film starts for everybody at the same moment.

The link did not open the app? Ask the host for the key instead: copy it, open Golether, paste it into the “Invitation” field and press “Connect”. A link works for 15 minutes, a key for up to an hour, and each of them is for one guest.

You invite

Show a film to your friends

The film stays on your computer and goes straight to your guests. Up to five people watch together.

  1. Pick the film

    Press “Create a session” and choose a video file. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

  2. Send the invitation

    Press “Invite”, copy the link and send it in any messenger. One link per guest.

  3. Let them in

    Compare the code with your guest by voice, admit them and press play.
